English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch

Asia:
Urdu, Pashto, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay

Europe:
Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Romanian, Greek, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish

Africa:
Swahili, Amharic, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Zulu, Afrikaans

Additional languages may be added without changing the architecture.
